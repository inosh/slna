SLNA Journal

The SLNA Journal is the Association's official publication, featuring research articles, clinical case studies, and professional commentary from Sri Lankan nurses and healthcare researchers. It is published periodically with peer-reviewed content and is open for member submissions throughout the year.

Aims & Scope

  • Advance evidence-based nursing practice across Sri Lanka
  • Provide a platform for members to publish original research and clinical experience
  • Share professional commentary on nursing education, policy, and leadership
  • Support the Association's contribution to nursing knowledge through the ICN network

Article Types

Original Research

Peer-reviewed primary studies.

Case Studies

Clinical case reports and lessons learned.

Review Articles

Literature reviews on focused topics.

Professional Commentary

Opinion and practice perspectives.

Editorial Process

1

Submission

Manuscripts are submitted to the editorial team for initial screening.

2

Editorial screening

Checked for scope, originality, and formatting compliance.

3

Peer review

Reviewed by subject-matter reviewers for clinical and academic rigour.

4

Decision & publication

Accepted articles are edited, typeset, and published in the next issue.
