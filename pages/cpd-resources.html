CPD Resources

A curated library of CPD resources, including clinical guidelines, reading materials, and reference documents, will support members' ongoing professional development.

SLNA's approach: Resources are gathered from SLNA's own events, publications, and trusted professional bodies, and organised here so members can find them in one place.

What You'll Find Here

  • Clinical guidelines and evidence-based practice documents
  • CPD point tracking sheets and personal learning logs
  • Reference materials drawn from past conferences and workshops
  • Templates for reflective practice and portfolio development
  • Links to recognised national and international nursing resources
