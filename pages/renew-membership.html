Renew Membership

Keep your SLNA membership active to continue enjoying uninterrupted access to CPD programmes, publications, and member benefits.

  • Renewals open annually ahead of the membership cycle
  • Payment accepted via bank transfer, online payment, or directly at the Secretariat
  • Renewal reminders are sent by email and SMS
  • Contact the Secretariat for assistance with lapsed memberships
