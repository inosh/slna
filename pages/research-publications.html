Submission Guidelines

1

Prepare your manuscript

Format your paper according to the SLNA Journal submission guidelines.

2

Submit for review

Email your manuscript and required declarations to research@slna.lk.

3

Peer review

Submissions undergo double-blind review by subject-matter reviewers.

4

Publication

Accepted papers are published here and in the SLNA Journal.

Submission requirements: original, unpublished work; institutional or ethics review committee clearance where applicable; a structured abstract of no more than 250 words; and references formatted in Vancouver style.
