Annual Conference

The SLNA Annual Conference is the Association's flagship event, bringing together nurses, researchers, and healthcare leaders from across Sri Lanka for several days of learning, professional exchange, and networking.

  • Keynote addresses from local and international speakers
  • Research paper and poster presentations by SLNA members
  • An awards ceremony recognising outstanding contributions to nursing
