Nursing Research

SLNA promotes evidence-based nursing practice by supporting research initiatives across Sri Lanka, contributing to the broader body of nursing knowledge advanced globally through the International Council of Nurses (ICN) and its member associations.

  • Research grants and support available for member-led studies
  • Collaboration with universities and healthcare institutions on nursing research
  • An annual research symposium with abstract competitions for members
