Nursing Research

SLNA promotes evidence-based nursing practice by supporting research initiatives across Sri Lanka, contributing to the broader body of nursing knowledge advanced globally through the International Council of Nurses (ICN) and its member associations.

Priority Research Areas

Members are encouraged to pursue research aligned with the following priority areas:

Clinical Practice

Patient Safety & Quality Care

Evidence-based practice, clinical outcomes, and infection prevention across care settings.

Public Health

Community & Public Health Nursing

Health promotion, disease prevention, and outreach in community settings.

Education

Nursing Education & Workforce

Curriculum development, competency training, and workforce planning.

Mental Health

Mental Health & Psychosocial Care

Mental health nursing practice and psychosocial support models.

Policy

Health Policy & Leadership

Nursing workforce policy, regulation, and leadership development.

Innovation

Digital Health & Innovation

Health informatics, telehealth, and technology-enabled care delivery.

Support for Researchers

  • Research grants and funding support for member-led studies
  • Collaboration with universities and healthcare institutions on nursing research
  • Guidance on research ethics and study design from the Research Committee
  • An annual research symposium with abstract submissions and presentation opportunities for members

Getting Involved

1

Identify a research question

Choose a topic aligned with one of SLNA's priority research areas.

2

Consult the Research Committee

Get guidance on study design, ethics requirements, and available support.

3

Apply for grants or mentorship

Access available funding, university partnerships, or peer mentorship.

4

Share your findings

Present at the Annual Research Symposium and submit to the SLNA Journal.

Research Ethics: All studies involving patients, staff, or SLNA members must obtain appropriate institutional or ethics review committee clearance before data collection begins, and must follow recognised standards for research integrity and participant confidentiality.

Contact the Research Committee

For guidance on research proposals, grant applications, or collaboration opportunities, email research@slna.lk or reach out via the Contact page.
