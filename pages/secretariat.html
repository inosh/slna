Secretariat

The SLNA Secretariat manages the daily administrative operations of the Association, serving as the first point of contact for members and the public.

  • Processes membership applications and annual renewals
  • Coordinates CPD programmes, conferences, and events nationwide
  • Manages official correspondence, records, and documentation
  • Located at the SLNA head office in Colombo
