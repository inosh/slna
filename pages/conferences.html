Conferences

SLNA organises national and regional conferences that bring together nurses, academics, and healthcare leaders to share knowledge, research, and best practice.

  • The Annual Conference is SLNA's flagship event, featuring keynote speakers and research presentations
  • Opportunities for abstract submission and poster presentations by members
  • Structured networking sessions with healthcare policymakers and institutional leaders
