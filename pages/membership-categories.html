Membership Categories & Fees

Simple, Affordable, Built Around Your Career

SLNA keeps membership straightforward with two categories that follow a nurse's career from training through to professional practice — a low-cost Associate Membership for nursing students, and a one-time Lifetime Membership for registered nurses. Fees are reviewed periodically by the Executive Committee and formally approved at the Annual General Meeting.

For Registered Nurses

Lifetime Member

Rs. 6,000One-time payment, no renewals
  • Open to nurses registered with the Sri Lanka Nursing Council (SLNC)
  • Pay once — membership is valid for life, with nothing further to renew
  • Full voting rights and access to every SLNA member benefit
  • Includes your official SLNA membership identity card
Apply for Lifetime Membership
For Nursing Students

Associate Member

Rs. 3,000For the duration of your studies
  • Open to nursing students enrolled at a recognised nursing school, college, or university
  • Covers the full period of pre-registration training
  • Access to CPD resources, SLNA updates, and the student community
  • A direct pathway to Lifetime Membership once you register as a nurse

Frequently Asked Questions

1

Is Lifetime Membership really a one-time fee?

Yes. The Rs. 6,000 fee is paid once, and your membership remains valid for your entire nursing career — there are no annual renewal fees.

2

What happens when I graduate and register with the SLNC?

You remain an Associate Member until you choose to upgrade. Once registered with the SLNC, you can upgrade to Lifetime Membership for an additional fee to be announced.

3

Can membership fees change?

Fees are reviewed periodically by the Executive Committee and any changes are formally approved at the Annual General Meeting.

4

What do I need to apply?

Your SLNC registration details (for Lifetime Membership), a bank-transfer receipt, and a passport-size photograph for your SLNA identity card.

5

How do I apply as an Associate Member?

Online applications currently support Lifetime Membership only. To apply as an Associate Member, contact the SLNA Secretariat and we'll guide you through the process.
