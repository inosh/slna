Membership Categories

SLNA offers several membership categories designed to accommodate registered nurses at different stages of their careers, from newly qualified nurses to those practising overseas.

CategoryEligibility
Full MemberRegistered nurses currently practising in Sri Lanka
Associate MemberNursing students and trainees
Retired MemberRetired registered nurses
Overseas MemberSri Lankan nurses practising abroad
Honorary MemberDistinguished individuals recognised by the Executive Committee for outstanding contribution to nursing
