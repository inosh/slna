Membership Categories & Fees

Simple, Affordable, Built Around Your Career

SLNA membership follows a nurse's career from training through to professional practice, with three categories: Associate Membership for nursing students, Ordinary Membership for registered nurses who prefer to pay each year, and a one-time Lifetime Membership. Fees are reviewed periodically by the Executive Committee and formally approved at the Annual General Meeting.

For Registered Nurses

Lifetime Member

Rs. 6,000One-time payment, no renewals
  • Open to nurses registered with the Sri Lanka Nursing Council (SLNC)
  • Pay once — membership is valid for life, with nothing further to renew
  • Full voting rights and access to every SLNA member benefit
  • Includes your official SLNA membership identity card
Apply for Lifetime Membership
For Registered Nurses

Ordinary Member

Rs. 1,200Per year, renewed annually
  • Open to nurses registered with the Sri Lanka Nursing Council (SLNC)
  • Valid for 12 months from approval — renew online each year
  • Access to every SLNA member benefit while your membership is current
  • Upgrade to Lifetime Membership at any time
Apply for Ordinary Membership
For Nursing Students

Associate Member

Rs. 3,000Per year, renewed annually
  • Open to nursing students enrolled at a recognised nursing school, college, or university
  • Valid for 12 months from approval — renew online each year of your studies
  • Access to CPD resources, SLNA updates, and the student community
  • Upgrade to Ordinary or Lifetime Membership once you register as a nurse

Associate Membership carries you through your studies at an affordable rate, with a clear upgrade route once you qualify.

Upgrading your membership: Associate Members can upgrade to Ordinary or Lifetime Membership, and Ordinary Members can upgrade to Lifetime Membership, at any time from the Renew Membership page. The upgrade fee is the fee of the new category; long-standing Ordinary Members may qualify for a reduced Lifetime upgrade fee, shown on the Renew Membership page. When you upgrade, you receive a new membership number in the new category.

Frequently Asked Questions

1

Is Lifetime Membership really a one-time fee?

Yes. The Rs. 6,000 fee is paid once, and your membership remains valid for your entire nursing career — there are no annual renewal fees.

2

How do I renew my Ordinary or Associate Membership?

We email you before your membership expires with a personal renewal link. You can also renew at any time on the Renew Membership page with your membership number. Renewing before it expires (or within the grace period) keeps your dates continuous.

3

What happens when I graduate and register with the SLNC?

You can upgrade your Associate Membership to Ordinary or Lifetime Membership from the Renew Membership page, paying the fee of the new category.

4

Can membership fees change?

Fees are reviewed periodically by the Executive Committee and any changes are formally approved at the Annual General Meeting.

5

What do I need to apply?

Your SLNC registration details (registered nurses) or your student number (nursing students), a bank-transfer receipt, and a passport-size photograph for your SLNA identity card.

6

I'm already a member. Do I apply again?

No. To renew or change your category, use the Renew Membership page. If you have lost your membership number, use "Find My Membership Number" on the Join SLNA page.
