CPD Programmes

SLNA runs a structured Continuing Professional Development (CPD) programme to help nurses maintain and enhance their clinical and professional competencies, in keeping with international standards for nursing education promoted by the International Council of Nurses (ICN).

  • Accredited CPD points count toward professional registration renewal requirements
  • Programmes cover clinical practice, leadership, ethics, and research topics
  • Delivered through in-person workshops, live webinars, and structured conferences
  • CPD progress can be tracked through the member portal
