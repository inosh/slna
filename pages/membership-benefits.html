Membership Benefits

Your Voice. Your Profession. Our Collective Strength.

SLNA members enjoy a wide range of benefits designed to support their professional growth, wellbeing, and standing within the global nursing community — from continuing professional development and networking to advocacy, recognition, and exclusive discounts.

Membership Benefits

PD

Professional Development

Access to CPD programmes, workshops, conferences, and training opportunities.

NC

Networking & Collaboration

Connect with a strong professional network, locally and internationally.

RA

Representation & Advocacy

Your voice for better working conditions, nursing rights, and policy influence.

RS

Resources & Support

Access to the latest information, research, guidelines, and professional resources.

RL

Recognition & Leadership

Opportunities to contribute, lead, and be recognised at national and international platforms.

%

Discounts & Special Offers

Enjoy benefits from partner organisations and educational institutions.

  • Discounted or free access to CPD programmes and the Annual Conference
  • Subscription to the SLNA Journal and regular Newsletters
  • Eligibility for scholarships, fellowships, and professional awards
  • A voice in SLNA's policy and advocacy positions affecting the profession

International Validity

SLNA membership enhances your professional recognition and opens doors to global opportunities through direct collaboration with renowned international nursing organisations. Your professional credentials, beyond borders.

Global Voice of Nursing

International Council of Nurses (ICN)

SLNA is proud to be in direct collaboration with the ICN, giving members access to global networks, resources, and professional opportunities.

Commonwealth Network

Commonwealth Nurses and Midwives Federation

SLNA is a full member of the Commonwealth Nurses and Midwives Federation, connecting Sri Lankan nurses with a wider Commonwealth community.
