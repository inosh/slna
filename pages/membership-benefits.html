Membership Benefits

SLNA members enjoy a wide range of benefits designed to support their professional growth, wellbeing, and standing within the global nursing community.

  • Discounted or free access to CPD programmes and the Annual Conference
  • Subscription to the SLNA Journal and regular Newsletters
  • Eligibility for scholarships, fellowships, and professional awards
  • Representation within the International Council of Nurses (ICN) global network
  • A voice in SLNA's policy and advocacy positions affecting the profession
