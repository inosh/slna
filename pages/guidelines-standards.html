Guidelines & Standards

SLNA publishes clinical and professional guidelines to support consistent, high-quality nursing practice nationwide, aligned where relevant with the standards set by the Sri Lanka Nurses Council under Act No. 19 of 1988.

  • Clinical practice guidelines organised by specialty
  • Professional conduct and ethics standards for members
  • Periodically reviewed and updated by expert panels within the Association
