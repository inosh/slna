Vision & Mission

SLNA's vision and mission are rooted in the founding motto "Unity for Quality" and align closely with the global mission of the International Council of Nurses (ICN): to represent nursing worldwide, advance the profession, promote the wellbeing of nurses, and advocate for health in all policies.

  • Vision: To be the recognised national leader in advancing nursing excellence, professional standards, and ethical practice across Sri Lanka's healthcare system
  • Mission: To empower nurses through continuous professional development, strong advocacy, and unwavering professional support
  • Core Values: Integrity, Compassion, Excellence, Advocacy, and Collaboration
  • Global alignment: SLNA's objectives mirror the ICN's founding aims of raising nursing education standards and professional ethics for the benefit of the public

Why "Unity for Quality"?

The motto adopted at SLNA's founding in 1943 reflects a dual commitment that remains central to the Association's work today: unity among nurses as a professional community, and an unwavering focus on the quality of care nurses provide to patients and communities across Sri Lanka.
