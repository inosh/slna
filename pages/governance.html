Governance

SLNA is governed by its constitution, which defines the Association's organisational structure, election procedures, and operational policies, ensuring transparency and accountability at every level of leadership.

  • Governed by the SLNA Constitution and by-laws, reviewed periodically by the membership
  • The Annual General Meeting (AGM) is the Association's highest decision-making body, held annually for elections and major resolutions
  • The Executive Committee, elected by the general membership, oversees day-to-day governance and strategic direction
  • Sub-committees manage specific functional areas including CPD, research, advocacy, and finance
  • Nurse registration, education standards, and disciplinary matters fall under the separate statutory Sri Lanka Nurses Council (Act No. 19 of 1988, amended by Act No. 35 of 2005), not SLNA directly

International Governance Alignment

As a member association of the International Council of Nurses (ICN) — a federation of over 140 national nursing associations representing more than 30 million nurses worldwide — SLNA's governance structures broadly reflect internationally recognised standards for professional nursing bodies, including democratic election of leadership and regular reporting to the membership.
