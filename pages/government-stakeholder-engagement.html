Government & Stakeholder Engagement

SLNA maintains active dialogue with government bodies, healthcare institutions, and international nursing organisations to represent the profession's interests at every level.

  • Regular engagement with the Ministry of Health on nursing regulations and workforce planning
  • Collaboration with the Sri Lanka Nurses Council on matters of shared interest
  • Active partnership within the International Council of Nurses (ICN), spanning over 140 national nursing associations worldwide
