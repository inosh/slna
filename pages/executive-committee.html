Executive Committee

The SLNA Executive Committee is elected by the general membership at the Annual General Meeting and is responsible for the strategic direction and day-to-day operational oversight of the Association.

  • Elected democratically by the general membership
  • Serves a defined term as set out in the SLNA Constitution
  • Oversees strategic planning, finances, and major Association decisions between AGMs
