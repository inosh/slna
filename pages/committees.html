Standing Committees

The Sri Lanka Nurses' Association operates through standing committees that support the Association's professional, administrative, welfare, planning, financial, and public-engagement responsibilities.

Following an Annual General Meeting, members are appointed to the standing committees at the first General Meeting. Each committee consists of five members and includes an officer of the Executive Committee. Select a committee below to view its role and appointed members.

01

Standing Committee

Nursing Education Committee

The Nursing Education Committee supports the advancement of nursing education, continuing professional development, professional learning, and initiatives that strengthen the knowledge and skills of nurses in Sri Lanka.
