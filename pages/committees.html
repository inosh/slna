Sub Committees

The Sri Lanka Nurses' Association operates through sub committees that support the Association's professional, administrative, welfare, planning, financial, and public-engagement responsibilities.

Following an Annual General Meeting, members are appointed to the sub committees at the first General Meeting. Each committee consists of five members and includes an officer of the Executive Committee. Select a committee below to view its role and appointed members.

Educational Service Committee

The Educational Service Committee supports the advancement of nursing education, continuing professional development, and initiatives that strengthen the knowledge and clinical skills of nurses across Sri Lanka.
