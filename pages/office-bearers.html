Office Bearers

SLNA's office bearers oversee key functional areas of the Association, ensuring smooth day-to-day operations across membership services, finance, publications, and events.

  • President and Vice President provide overall leadership and represent SLNA externally
  • Honorary Secretary and Assistant Secretary manage correspondence and administrative continuity
  • Honorary Treasurer oversees the Association's finances and annual budget
  • Editor of the SLNA Journal manages the Association's official publication
  • Committee Members represent SLNA's regional branches across the island
