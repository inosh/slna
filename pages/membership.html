Membership

Together for Professional Excellence and a Healthier Nation

SLNA membership connects registered nurses across Sri Lanka with a national professional network, offering access to continuing professional development, advocacy support, publications, and exclusive member benefits. Because SLNA is a full member of the International Council of Nurses (ICN), every SLNA member is automatically represented within the world's largest international federation of nursing associations — the ICN does not offer individual membership, only membership through recognised national associations like SLNA.

Why Join SLNA?

Strengthen your professional journey with opportunities for development, networking, representation, leadership, recognition, and valuable professional resources.

Who Can Join

  • Open to all registered nurses practising in Sri Lanka
  • Multiple membership categories to suit different career stages, from students to retirees
  • Access to CPD programmes, the SLNA Journal, and professional resources
  • A collective, organised voice representing nursing interests nationally and internationally
