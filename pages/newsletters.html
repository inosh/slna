Newsletters

Stay informed with SLNA's regular newsletters covering Association updates, member achievements, and upcoming events across the country.

What's Inside Each Issue

  • President's message and Association updates
  • Member achievements and branch spotlights
  • Upcoming CPD events and programmes
  • Branch and regional news
  • Policy and advocacy updates

Publication schedule: Newsletters are issued quarterly (January, April, July, and October) and sent to all active members by email, with the archive available on this page.
