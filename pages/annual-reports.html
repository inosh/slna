Annual Reports

SLNA's Annual Reports provide a comprehensive overview of the Association's activities, finances, and achievements each year, presented for approval at the Annual General Meeting.

What's Included

  • President's and Secretary's reports
  • Financial statements and audit summary
  • Membership statistics and growth
  • Branch and regional activity reports
  • CPD and events summary
  • Committee reports

Approval Process

1

Report compiled

Drafted by the Secretariat with input from the Executive Committee.

2

Financial review

Financial statements are reviewed by the auditors and Finance Committee.

3

Circulated to members

Shared with members ahead of the Annual General Meeting.

4

Presented & adopted

Presented and formally adopted at the AGM each year.
