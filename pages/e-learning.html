Online Learning

The SLNA Online Learning portal will provide self-paced e-learning courses and resources, allowing members to pursue continuous professional growth flexibly around clinical shift schedules.

SLNA's approach: Online Learning modules are designed to complement in-person CPD events and workshops, giving members a flexible way to build knowledge and earn CPD points between activities.

What the Portal Will Offer

  • Self-paced modules with built-in assessments
  • CPD points awarded automatically on successful completion
  • Accessible any time through the Members' area of the SLNA website
  • Courses covering clinical practice, leadership, ethics, and emerging topics in nursing
  • Progress tracking and downloadable certificates of completion
