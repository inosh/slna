President's Message

A message from the President

It is my privilege to welcome you to the Sri Lanka Nurses' Association. Since 1943, SLNA has represented and supported nurses who serve communities across our country with knowledge, skill, compassion, and professional integrity.

Our commitment is to strengthen nursing through continuing professional development, advocacy for the welfare and rights of nurses, and the promotion of high standards in education, ethical practice, and patient care. Together, we will continue to build a confident, respected, and future-ready nursing profession for Sri Lanka.

Mr. W. A. Keerthirathne
President, Sri Lanka Nurses' Association
