President's Message

SLNA

As President of the Sri Lanka Nurses' Association, I am proud to lead an organisation dedicated to elevating the standards of nursing practice, education, and welfare across the country. Since our founding in 1943 under the motto "Unity for Quality," and building on a professional nursing tradition in Sri Lanka that dates back to 1878 — when the first Nightingale-trained nurses arrived in colonial Ceylon — we remain committed to advocacy, continuous professional development, and strengthening the voice of nurses at every level of the healthcare system, both nationally and as part of the global nursing community through the International Council of Nurses.

— President, SLNA
